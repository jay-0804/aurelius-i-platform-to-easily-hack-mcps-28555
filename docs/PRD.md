**Product Requirements Document (PRD)**
=====================================

**Vision**
-----------

Our vision is to create a platform that empowers small companies to easily manage and optimize their Minimum Viable Products (MVPs) and Minimum Credible Products (MCPs), enabling them to innovate and grow rapidly. We aim to provide a user-friendly and intuitive platform that simplifies the process of defining, building, and launching successful products.

**Problem**
------------

Small companies often struggle to define and build successful products due to limited resources, lack of expertise, and inefficient processes. The current methods for defining and building MCPs are time-consuming, costly, and prone to errors. As a result, many small companies fail to launch successful products, leading to wasted resources and missed opportunities.

**Solution**
------------

Our platform will provide a comprehensive solution for small companies to easily define, build, and launch successful MCPs. The platform will offer a range of tools and features that simplify the product development process, including:

* A user-friendly interface for defining product requirements and specifications
* A library of pre-built templates and examples for common product types
* A collaboration tool for stakeholders to provide input and feedback
* A project management system for tracking progress and timelines
* Integration with popular development tools and platforms

**Target Users**
----------------

Our target users are small companies with fewer than 100 employees, across various industries, including:

* Software development
* E-commerce
* Digital marketing
* Product design
* Consulting

**MVP Features**
----------------

For our Minimum Viable Product (MVP) release, we will focus on the following key features:

1. **Product Requirements Template**: A customizable template for defining product requirements and specifications
2. **Collaboration Tool**: A real-time collaboration tool for stakeholders to provide input and feedback
3. **Project Management System**: A basic project management system for tracking progress and timelines
4. **Integration with Trello**: Integration with Trello for seamless project management

**Metrics**
------------

To measure the success of our platform, we will track the following key metrics:

1. **User Acquisition**: The number of new users signing up for the platform
2. **User Engagement**: The frequency and duration of user sessions on the platform
3. **Customer Satisfaction**: The overall satisfaction of users with the platform, measured through surveys and feedback
4. **Conversion Rate**: The percentage of users who successfully launch a product using the platform

**Roadmap**
------------

Our roadmap for the next 6 months includes:

1. **MVP Release** (Week 1-4): Release the MVP version of the platform with the key features outlined above
2. **User Feedback and Iteration** (Week 5-8): Collect user feedback and iterate on the platform to improve user experience and functionality
3. **Additional Feature Development** (Week 9-12): Develop and release additional features, including integration with other popular development tools and platforms
4. **Marketing and Promotion** (Week 13-24): Launch marketing and promotion campaigns to acquire new users and increase brand awareness

**User Stories**
----------------

Here are some example user stories that illustrate the key features and functionality of the platform:

1. **As a product manager, I want to be able to define product requirements and specifications using a customizable template, so that I can ensure that my product meets the needs of my customers.**
2. **As a developer, I want to be able to collaborate with stakeholders in real-time, so that I can get feedback and input on my product development.**
3. **As a project manager, I want to be able to track progress and timelines, so that I can ensure that my project is on schedule and within budget.**
4. **As a user, I want to be able to integrate the platform with my existing development tools and platforms, so that I can streamline my workflow and increase productivity.**

**Competitors**
---------------

Our research has identified no direct competitors in the market, providing a unique opportunity for our platform to fill a significant gap in the industry.

**Opportunity**
---------------

The opportunity for our platform is significant, with a large and growing market of small companies that need help defining and building successful products. By providing a comprehensive and user-friendly platform, we can capture a significant share of this market and establish ourselves as a leader in the industry.
